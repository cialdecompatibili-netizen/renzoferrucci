<p>Naomi Campbell is one of the most famous and influential supermodels in the history of fashion. Her beauty, her charisma and her personality have made her a true icon, admired and followed by millions of people around the world. But how much do you really know Naomi Campbell? In this article, we offer you a list of frequently asked questions and answers about his life, his career and his interests. Let's find out together who Naomi Campbell is and why she is the most loved supermodel.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Chi_e_Naomi_Campbell"></span>Who is Naomi Campbell?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Naomi Campbell is a <strong>supermodel</strong>, English actress, entrepreneur and activist, born in London on 22 May 1970. She is considered one of the <em>"Big Six"</em>, the six most famous models of the 90s, together with Cindy Crawford, Claudia Schiffer, Christy Turlington, Linda Evangelista and Kate Moss. She has modeled for the greatest designers and fashion brands, such as Chanel, Dior, Versace, Prada, Gucci and many others. She has also posed for the covers of the most prestigious fashion magazines, such as Vogue, Elle, Harper's Bazaar and Vanity Fair. He has also acted in several films and TV series, such as <em>The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air</em>, <em>Zoolander</em>, <em>Empire</em> and <em>American Horror Story</em>. She is also the founder of a beauty line called <em>Naomi Campbell Beauty</em> and a digital platform dedicated to African fashion called <em>No Filter with Naomi</em>.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Come_ha_iniziato_la_sua_carriera_da_modella"></span>How did you start your modeling career?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Naomi Campbell began her modeling career at just 15 years old, when she was discovered by an agent while shopping in a London mall. Her first photo shoot was for the British magazine <em>Elle</em>, in 1986. Since then, her popularity grew rapidly and she became one of the most sought-after models of the moment. His debut on the catwalk took place in 1987, for the Azzedine Alaïa fashion show in Paris. In 1988, she was the first black model to appear on the cover of the French edition of <em>Vogue</em>, thanks to the intervention of stylist Yves Saint Laurent. In 1989, she was also the first black model to appear on the cover of the American edition of <em>Vogue</em>, for the issue dedicated to supermodels. In 1991, she became part of the group of <em>"Big Six"</em>, the six most famous models of the 90s, who dominated the catwalks and fashion advertising campaigns.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Quali_sono_i_suoi_rapporti_con_gli_altri_personaggi_della_moda"></span>What are your relationships with other fashion figures?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Naomi Campbell has had friendships and rivalries with many fashion personalities. Among her closest friends are the other supermodels in her group, in particular Christy Turlington and Linda Evangelista, with whom she formed the trio nicknamed <em>“The Trinity”</em>. The three models were inseparable and supported each other in their work. Naomi Campbell also had a romantic relationship with Mike Tyson in 1989 and with Robert De Niro in 1993. Among her declared enemies are Tyra Banks, with whom she had a long professional feud due to their physical resemblance, and Ugo Cacciatori, an Italian stylist who accused her of stealing some jewels from him in 2005.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Cosa_fa_oggi_Naomi_Campbell"></span>What is Naomi Campbell doing today?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Today Naomi Campbell continues to be one of the most active and sought-after models in the fashion world. She modeled for the spring/summer 2022 collections of Balmain, Versace and Alexander McQueen, proving that she has not lost her charm and elegance. She also launched a digital platform called <em>No Filter with Naomi</em>, in which she interviews other famous people in fashion, art, music and entertainment, such as Anna Wintour, Cindy Crawford, Marc Jacobs and Oprah Winfrey. Additionally, she became a mother for the first time in May 2021, via a surrogate mother. She announced the birth of her daughter with a post on Instagram, in which she wrote: <em>"A beautiful blessing has chosen me as her mother. I am so honored to have this sweet soul in my life. There are no words to describe the bond I now share with you, my angel. There is no greater love"</em>.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Quali_sono_le_sue_passioni_e_i_suoi_interessi"></span>What are your passions and interests?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Naomi Campbell is a woman of many passions and interests. Among these, there are music, cinema, photography, cooking, travel and yoga. She is also a great fan of African art and African fashion, which she promotes through her digital platform and humanitarian projects. Naomi Campbell is in fact a fervent activist, who is committed to various social and environmental causes. Among its most important initiatives are <em>Fashion for Relief</em>, a foundation that organizes charity fashion shows to raise funds for victims of natural and humanitarian disasters, and <em>Naomi Campbell Design for Life</em>, a campaign that supports the professional training of young African designers.</p>

She is a strong, determined and generous woman, who was able to overcome difficulties and criticism with courage and dignity. She is also a loving mother, a tastemaker and a source of inspiration for millions of people. Naomi Campbell is the most loved supermodel because she is unique and unrepeatable.</p>
