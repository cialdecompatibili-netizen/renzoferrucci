<p>Seeing it now shining among the most famous fashion magazines and in the hands of thousands of readers of all age groups, we would find it hard to believe that until a few years ago its editorial fate seemed rather uncertain. Born from the ashes of <em>Tu</em>, <strong><em>Tu Style</em></strong> it is a fresh weekly magazine that speaks to women not only about the latest fashion trends but also about delicate issues, as well as opening an unprecedented window on the lives of Italian and international stars.</p>
<p>Since its lively cover, <em>Tu Style</em> manages to capture the attention of a large portion of the female public (and not only) regardless of age, lifestyle and even wallet, also thanks to an affordable price. We know better the history of this extraordinary <strong> Italian product </strong>.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Storia_di_Tu_Style"></span>History of <em>Tu Style</em><span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>The weekly <em><strong>Tu Style</strong></em> was founded on <strong>24 November 1999</strong>. At the beginning the weekly was simply called <strong><em>Tu</em></strong> and was directed by <strong>Marisa Demichei</strong>; the magazine is characterized not only by its immediate style and therefore close to every type of reader, but above all by a <strong> cost </strong> that is, to say the least, <strong> popular </strong>.</p>
<p>The magazine subsequently changed its name, renaming itself <em>Tu Style</em>. In February 2009, the release date of the first issue, <em>Tu Style</em> could be purchased on newsstands for only <strong>fifty cents</strong>. The intent of his publishing house, <strong>Mondadori</strong>, was to increase sales and thus broaden the segment of the female audience.</p>
<h3><span class="ez-toc-section" id="2018_la_crisi_e_il_rischio_chiusura"></span>2018: the crisis and the risk of closure<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>
<p>Despite Mondadori's noble intentions,<strong><em> Tu Style</em></strong> experienced a <strong>critical moment in 2018</strong>, when the publishing house began to seriously evaluate the sale of the weekly.</p>
<p>This intention, however, was abandoned almost immediately: Mondadori decided to keep ownership of Tu Style at the cost, however, of a significant cut in the salaries of its employees who managed to keep their jobs. <strong><em>Tu Style</em> begins to climb back up</strong>, also thanks to the sales of digital copies: the magazine continues its renewal: a restyling that rewards, with an increase in sales.</p>
<p>The director of the weekly in 2018 is <strong> Annalisa Monfreda </strong>.</p>
<h3><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Cambio_al_vertice_2019"></span>Change at the top (2019)<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>
<p><strong>2019</strong> is an important year for <em>Tu Style</em>. The magazine was sold to <strong>Maurizio Belpietro</strong>, former director of the newspaper <em>La Verità</em>, who for the occasion created a new company, <strong>Stile Italia Edizioni</strong>.</p>
<p>The new publishing house, made up of 25 percent by Mondadori and with the majority in the hands of <strong>La Verità Srl</strong>, gives new life to the magazine. <strong>Belpietro takes over the direction of the magazine</strong>.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Iniziative_e_collaborazioni"></span>Initiatives and collaborations<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><em><strong>Tu Style</strong></em> has undertaken <strong>important collaborations</strong>, showing that it is at the forefront of promoting the fashion sector, encouraging the "new generation" in the fashion field to continue pursuing their objectives and sponsoring important local events.</p>
<ul>
<li>In <strong>2017</strong> the magazine is media partner of the <strong>Salon du chocolat</strong> in Milan. During the event, the weekly magazine then published by Mondadori donated <strong> one hundred free passes </strong> to attend an exclusive <strong> opening night </strong> in which some of the greatest personalities of Italian cuisine took part;</li>
<li>&nbsp;In <strong>2018</strong> an <strong>important partnership </strong>takes shape between <strong><em>Tu Style </em></strong>and the<strong> MKS Milano Fashion School – Istituto di Moda</strong> which results in the launch of the <strong><em>Tustyle Fashion School</em></strong> competition. The contest offers a significant fashion stylist scholarship. To promote the initiative <em>Tu Style</em> used three students from the school to create a video tutorial on how to create a look.</li>
</ul>
