<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Sergio_Galeotti"></span>Sergio Galeotti<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Sergio Galeotti</strong> was a great Italian entrepreneur, best known for being the co-founder of Giorgio Armani together with the famous stylist<strong> <a href="{{ '/stilisti/stilisti-italiani/giorgio-armani/' | relative_url }}">Giorgio Armani</a></strong>. His life and career were characterized by successes and tragic losses, but his name remained inextricably linked to the history of Italian fashion.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Biografia"></span>Biography<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Sergio Galeotti</strong> was born on 26 July 1945 and grew up in Carrara, where he attended art school. After earning a degree in architecture from the University of Florence, he worked in several architectural firms in Italy.</p>
<p>In 1966, Galeotti met Giorgio Armani in Forte dei Marmi, Tuscany. The two began working together, with Galeotti primarily handling business management, while Armani focused on clothing design and production. In 1975, the two founded the Giorgio Armani Corporation, which would become one of the largest and most influential fashion companies in the world.</p>
<p>Galeotti was known for his ability to make deals and grow the company. He played an important role in the expansion of the Armani brand around the world, opening stores in several European, American and Asian cities. Additionally, he worked on creating a wide range of Armani products, including clothing, perfumes, watches, eyewear and home accessories.</p>
<p>However, Galeotti's life was ended prematurely by illness. In 1984, he was diagnosed with a serious form of leukemia, but <strong> continued to work at the helm of the company </strong> despite the illness. He was treated as soon as the disease was diagnosed at the Pasteur Institute in Paris and in New York but he did not make it, unfortunately, on 14 August 1985, Galeotti died of a heart attack in his home in Milan, at the age of just 40.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Collaborazione_con_Armani"></span>Collaboration with Armani<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>In 1975, Galeotti helped Armani found his company,<strong> <a href="{{ '/case-di-moda/armani-spa/' | relative_url }}">Giorgio Armani SpA</a></strong>, which would soon establish itself as one of the most important and influential brands in the Italian fashion industry.</p>
<p>Sergio Galeotti was one of the founders of the Giorgio Armani Corporation and collaborated closely with the famous Italian fashion designer Giorgio Armani. Together, the two developed a close friendship and created one of the most iconic and recognizable fashion brands in the world. Galeotti, an architect by training, first met Armani in 1966 in Forte dei Marmi, Tuscany, where the two began their collaboration.</p>
<p>The collaboration between Galeotti and Armani was crucial to the success of the company. Galeotti played an important role in creating Armani's image as a luxury brand and in expanding the company internationally. He also oversaw the management of the company and its subsidiaries. Galeotti was Armani's life partner and his sudden and premature death in 1985 had a significant impact on the company and the life of the famous Italian designer.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="I_successi"></span>Success<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Sergio Galeotti was one of the most influential and innovative stylists and businessmen in Italian fashion. Thanks to his collaboration with Giorgio Armani and his entrepreneurial vision, Galeotti contributed significantly to the creation of a fashion empire that is still recognized globally today.</p>
<p>Among Galeotti's successes in fashion was the creation of the Emporio Armani line, launched in 1981. The line was aimed at a young and dynamic audience, offering a fresh and modern alternative to Giorgio Armani's main line. The Emporio Armani line was a huge international success and became an independent brand in 1984.</p>
<p>Galeotti was also one of the first to understand the importance of marketing and branding in the fashion industry, and worked hard to create a globally recognized Italian style. Under his leadership, Giorgio Armani SpA expanded rapidly, opening new stores around the world and launching new clothing lines.</p>
<p>Thanks to his successes in fashion, Sergio Galeotti became a point of reference for the Italian fashion industry and his impact on the global fashion industry is still evident today.</p>
