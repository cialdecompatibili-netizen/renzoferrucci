<h2 data-fontsize="48" style="--fontSize:48; line-height: 1.2;" data-lineheight="57.6px" class="fusion-responsive-typography-calculated"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Biografia"></span>Biography<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Cara Jocelyn Delevingne</strong> was born on 12 August 1992 in London, Belgravia, the third of three sisters: <strong>Poppy</strong>, also a model and actress, and<strong> Chloe</strong>. The girl even boasts noble ancestry on her mother's side, related to the Faudel-Phillips barons.</p>
<p>Cara's zodiac sign, Leo, well reflects her liveliness and her desire to show off. She immediately followed in the footsteps of her sister <strong>Poppy Delevingne</strong>, starting her modeling career at 17. He abandons his studies and signs a contract with <strong>Storm Model Management</strong>. Her first appearance as a model was due to a photo published in “<strong>Love magazine</strong>“; his career from that moment became a rapid rise to success.</p>
<p>Delevingne paraded for the big names in international fashion: <strong>Versace, Chanel, Fendi, Burberry</strong> and was elected Model of the Year at the <strong>British Fashion Awards</strong> for two years - in 2012 and 2014. Furthermore, in 2014, the protest fashion show created by <strong>Karl Lagerfeld</strong> for Chanel also opened during Paris Fashion Week.</p>
<p>The supermodel <strong> retired from the catwalks </strong> in 2015. The reason for this farewell was first and foremost that of wanting to dedicate herself to <strong> acting </strong> (Delevingne began her career as an actress in parallel in 2012), but the very young Cara has never denied the <strong> physical complexes </strong> that her activity as a top model has created for her. In 2016, <strong>Madame Tussauds</strong> in London honored her with a wax statue.</p>
</p>

</p>

<h2 data-fontsize="48" style="--fontSize:48; line-height: 1.2;" data-lineheight="57.6px" class="fusion-responsive-typography-calculated"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Carriera_di_attrice"></span>Actress career<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>In 2015 Cara Delevingne decided to dedicate herself full time to the profession of <strong>actress</strong>, a career that the thirty-year-old had undertaken in parallel with that of a model.</p>
<p>The girl made her debut in 2012 in the film <em><strong>Anna Karenina</strong></em> by Joe Wright playing a minor role. The young Londoner has done a lot of work, taking part in various film and television productions and even lending her voice to some video games. Among her best-known roles are: <strong>Margo Roth Spiegelman</strong> in the drama <strong><em>Paper Towns</em></strong>, the <strong>Enchantress</strong> in the blockbuster <strong><em>Suicide Squad</em></strong> and <strong>Vignette Stonemoss</strong> in the fantasy TV series <strong><em>Carnival Row</em></strong>, at alongside <strong>Orlando Bloom</strong>.</p>

<p>Cara Delevingne's <strong> sentimental sphere </strong> has always been the subject of particular, often morbid, attention from the press.</p>
<p>Despite being under the eye of the storm, the blonde former model has never made a secret of being <strong>pansexual</strong>. Delevingne had two important relationships: the first, from 2015 to 2016, with the American singer <strong>St.Vincent</strong>; the second, much more "flattened" in gossip magazines, with the star of <em>Pretty Little Liars</em> <strong>Ashley Benson</strong>, lasted from 2018 to 2020.</p>
<p>Delevingne is highly appreciated by animal rights activists thanks to her <strong> commitment against the hunting of endangered animals </strong> with the ultimate aim of obtaining useless trophies. The star has in fact embraced and promoted the global campaign <em>I'm not a trophy</em>.</p>
