<p>If you are looking for fashion inspiration, I present to you some of the most followed and influential Italian fashion bloggers of 2023. Fashion is a constantly evolving sector and Italian fashion bloggers have been able to gain an important position in the fashion world thanks to their ability to influence consumer choices. Let's discover together the bloggers who are trending in 2023.</p>
<ol>
<li>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Chiara_Ferragni"></span>Chiara Ferragni<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Chiara Ferragni</strong> is a name that needs no introduction in the world of fashion and entertainment: born in Cremona in 1987, Chiara has become one of the most important and successful influencers in the world, with over <strong>23 million followers</strong> on <strong>Instagram</strong> and collaborations with the biggest fashion and lifestyle brands.</p>
<p>Her career in the fashion world began in 2009, when she founded her fashion blog, <a href="https://theblondesalad.com/"><strong><em>The Blonde Salad</em></strong></a>. In just a few years, the blog has become one of the most influential and popular in the world, and Chiara has been invited to the most important global fashion shows and major industry events.</p>
<p>Chiara Ferragni's success did not stop at the world of blogs, but extended to the creation of her personal fashion brand: <a href="https://it.chiaraferragnibrand.com/"><strong>Chiara Ferragni Brand</strong></a>. Becoming a huge commercial success, it sold worldwide and quickly became a must-have for fashionistas everywhere.</p>
<p>But our Chiara Ferragni is not only a fashion blogger and a successful entrepreneur, but also a social activist. She has supported numerous organizations and social causes, in fact, in 2019, together with her husband Fedez, she donated 1 million euros to the San Raffaele Hospital in Milan to help in the fight against COVID-19.</p>
<p>In 2021 it launched a make-up line marked by the unmistakable “<strong><em>Blinking eye</em></strong>” which you will surely have already seen on t-shirts and accessories.</p>
<p>Finally, for those who don't know, the TV series about the most social family, "The Ferragnez", was released on December 9th, a series available on <strong>Amazon Prime Video</strong>. And now you are wondering: "But how much does Chiara earn?". It is estimated that his assets are around 10 million euros!</p>
<p>Chiara Ferragni's story is an example of how passion, determination and hard work can lead to success in any field you choose to venture into. Chiara has demonstrated that success is not just a matter of luck, but of constant commitment and a great avant-garde vision.</p>
<p>In summary, therefore, Chiara Ferragni is one of the most important and successful influencers in the world, thanks to her career in the world of fashion, her creativity and the social commitment she demonstrates every day. If you are looking for a style icon and a successful figure to draw inspiration from, <strong>Chiara Ferragni is definitely a winning choice</strong>!</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Veronica_Ferraro"></span>Veronica Ferraro<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Veronica Ferraro</strong> is one of the most famous and appreciated influencers in the fashion world. Born in Milan in 1987, she founded the blog <strong><em>The Fashion Fruit</em></strong> in 2010 and since then she has won the hearts of millions of followers in Italy and abroad, becoming an important figure in the fashion industry.</p>
<p>Veronica Ferraro's blog is a point of reference for those looking for inspiration for their fashion outfits... Even for 4-legged friends! Thanks to her unique and original creations, Veronica Ferraro has become one of the most admired style icons, but her success is not limited only to fashion: Veronica Ferraro also deals with lifestyle, travel and culture, enriching her blog with always new and interesting content.</p>
<p>Veronica Ferraro's success is due to her great passion for fashion and her creativity, which led her to collaborate with the largest fashion and lifestyle brands in the world, including <strong>H&amp;M</strong>, <strong>Chanel</strong>, <strong>Dior</strong>, <strong>Louis Vuitton</strong> and many others. Thanks to these collaborations, Veronica Ferraro has become an important figure in the world of fashion and one of the best-known faces of Italian influencers.</p>
<p>Veronica Ferraro is not only a very successful fashion blogger, but also a social activist involved in numerous charitable causes. In 2021 he collaborated with <strong>UNICEF</strong> for the campaign "<strong>Io Sono Qui</strong>", raising public awareness of the importance of education for children around the world. Thanks to her active participation in the campaign, Veronica Ferraro has once again demonstrated her sensitivity towards social issues and her dedication to improving the world we live in. Following his fashion creations and his collaborations with the most important brands in the world also means supporting a figure who is committed and sensitive to social causes.</p>
<p>If you are looking for a style icon and a successful figure to draw inspiration from, Veronica Ferraro is certainly a winning choice. Her unique style and her passion for fashion make her a point of reference for those who want to stand out and create unique and original outfits.</p>
<p>If you want to follow her creations and her collaborations with the biggest brands in the world, don't miss the opportunity to visit her blog <em>The Fashion Fruit</em> and follow her on social media!</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Mariano_Di_Vaio"></span>Mariano Di Vaio<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Mariano Di Vaio</strong> is a famous Italian influencer who has achieved fame as a fashion blogger, designer, actor and model. Of Neapolitan-Calabrian origins, born in 1989 in Assisi, with over 8.4 million followers on social media, including 6.8 million on Instagram, Mariano Di Vaio is one of the most influential figures in the men's fashion industry.</p>
<p>Mariano Di Vaio is known for his breathtaking beauty and impeccable style: this has led him to work as a&nbsp;brand ambassador for various brands, including <strong>Hugo Boss</strong>, <strong>Dolce &amp; Gabbana</strong> and <strong>Calvin Klein</strong>.</p>
<p>His nature is typical of a fighter, always looking for new challenges and new goals to achieve: he created his own fashion line, <a href="https://mdvstyle.com/"><strong>MDV Style</strong></a>, which includes jewellery, <a href="https://mdvstyle.com/mdv-jewels/"><strong>MDV Jewels</strong></a>, and a collection of shoes, <a href="https://mdvstyle.com/mdv-shoes/"><strong>MDV Shoes</strong></a>.</p>
<p>But he is not only a successful entrepreneur, he is also a loving father: in 2022, in fact, he became the father of little <strong>Nathan Leone</strong>, who he had with his wife <strong>Eleonora Brunacci</strong>.</p>
<p>Despite his busy life, Mariano Di Vaio always pays attention to his health and beauty. In an interview with Glamor, she shared some of her personal care routines, including the importance of maintaining a healthy, balanced diet, drinking plenty of water, and exercising regularly.</p>
<p>In summary, Mariano Di Vaio is a major figure in the world of fashion and entertainment, known for his breathtaking beauty, his impeccable style and his determination to achieve his goals. With his focus on personal health and beauty, he is a positive example for many people around the world!</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Chiara_Biasi"></span>Chiara Biasi<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Chiara Biasi</strong>, a well-known Italian influencer, has conquered the public with her unique personality and her glamorous lifestyle. We explore his life, his career and why he has so many followers.</p>
<p>Chiara Biasi was born in Pordenone, Italy on March 28, 1990. After completing her studies, she began her career working as a hostess and model. In 2012, she decided to open a fashion blog, under the advice of Chiara Ferragni, which would become the launching pad for her career as an influencer.</p>
<p>Since then, Chiara Biasi has become one of the most famous influencers in Italy, with over 3.1 million followers on Instagram. She has collaborated with numerous fashion, beauty and lifestyle brands, and has become a style icon for many Italian women.</p>
<p>In addition to her activity as an influencer, Chiara has collaborated with numerous fashion and lifestyle brands, becoming an icon of style and elegance. In 2012, she collaborated with brands such as “<strong>Bikini Lovers</strong>” and “<strong>Pin up stars</strong>“, while today she is often called upon to represent international fashion and beauty brands.</p>
<p>In 2017, Chiara wrote a book for Mondadori, the biography “<a href="https://www.ibs.it/chiaroscuri-libro-chiara-biasi/e/9788891806758" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Chiaroscuri</strong></a>“, which tells her story and her passions, from her lifestyle blog to her success as an influencer and style icon.</p>
<p>Recently, she launched her own swimwear line, called “<a href="https://www.pinup-stars.com/it/i-nostri-brand/poisson-d-amour/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Poisson d'amour</strong></a>“, in collaboration with the fashion brand Pin Up Stars.</p>
<p>Chiara Biasi's swimwear line is inspired by current trends and offers a vast selection of fashionable and quality models, suitable for every type of need and physical shape. The costumes “<strong>Poisson d'amour</strong>” were appreciated by many celebrities, including Nicole Mazzocato, Chiara Giuffrida and Lara Rosie Zorzetto.</p>
<p>The “<strong>Poisson d'amour</strong>” collection is characterized by bright colors and original patterns, which adapt perfectly to the taste of Chiara Biasi, an icon of style and elegance. The collection includes one-piece swimsuits, bikinis, trikinis and many other models, all made with high quality fabrics and with particular attention to detail.<br />
In summary, Chiara Biasi is an important figure in the fashion scene in Italy, who has conquered the public thanks to her authenticity, passion for a healthy and balanced lifestyle and her elegance. If you are looking for inspiration for your lifestyle or want to follow one of the most famous influencers in Italy, you can't help but follow Chiara Biasi on social media and on her lifestyle blog!</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Eleonora_Carisi"></span>Eleonora Carisi<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Eleonora Carisi</strong> is a famous Italian fashion blogger and influencer, born in Turin in 1984. From a young age, Eleonora cultivated a great passion for fashion, thanks to her mother who owned a clothing store. From the age of 15 she moved to Milan to attend the Faculty of Marketing and Communication at the IED, working as a shop assistant and waitress in the meantime.</p>
<p>After obtaining her degree, Eleonora decides to open her own business in the field of commerce, taking over a craft creations shop in the center of Turin and inaugurating her <strong>You You Store</strong>, a small boutique offering clothing and accessories from Italian and foreign brands with a unique style far from the more well-known and renowned models.</p>
<p>But Eleonora isn't satisfied with just this: in 2009 she launched her first clothing line, <a href="https://whatsinsideyou.bigcartel.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>What's Inside You</strong></a>, created in collaboration with emerging young people in fashion, even going so far as to create a capsule collection with Zalando, Patrizia Pepe and Maria Grazia Severi.</p>
<p>Thanks to the notoriety achieved, Eleonora becomes a testimonial for numerous fashion and beauty brands, such as Michael Kors, Chanel, Moschino, L'Oreal, Gucci and Redken. In 2010, she decided to launch the online platform Jou Jou Villeroy, where with her posts, like in a diary, she tells what strikes her by combining personal thoughts and style advice. With Jou Jou Villeroy, Eleonora consolidates her status as an influencer, becoming one of the most followed fashion bloggers on social media.</p>
<p>Her popularity allows her to participate in the television program “<strong>The Influencer</strong>” on Real Time with Candela Pelizza. Currently, Eleonora is busy as founder and creative director of the online platform <a href="http://www.grumblecreative.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Grumblecreative</strong></a>.</p>
<p>Eleonora Carisi is an example of how a passion for fashion can turn into a successful career, thanks to determination, hard work and creativity. Its history demonstrates that it is possible to create your own personal and unique style, distinguishing yourself from the most common models and creating your own fashion brand, capable of conquering the public!</li>
