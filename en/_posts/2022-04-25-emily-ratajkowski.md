<p>Born in London but raised in California, <strong>Emily Ratajkowski</strong> is the daughter of an American literature teacher and a painter and art teacher. Young Emily leaves the UK for the US at five; since her early years of life the young woman has shown a particular propensity for the theatre.</p>
<p>Ratajkowski's <strong>career</strong> began very early, when at the age of fourteen she was put in contact with the <strong>Ford Models</strong> agency through an entertainment agent. The teenager Emily begins to pose for some catalogs and, at the same time, undergoes auditions for Disney and Nickelodeon series. This is the period in which the beautiful young woman was hired to play a part in the well-known TV series “<strong>iCarly</strong>“.</p>
<p>In 2009 Emily Ratajkowski put aside her university studies to launch herself into the <strong>glossy world of fashion</strong> and music videos, not disdaining "succinct" proposals such as posing for the erotic magazine "<strong>Treats!</strong>". The woman appears to be at ease without clothes on: since she was young, in fact, she has admitted to having visited numerous nudist beaches, not experiencing being naked as a taboo.</p>
<p>In 2015 Emily Ratajkowski officially debuted as a model on catwalks around the world. The supermodel parades for the big names in international fashion: <strong>Marc Jacobs, Miu Miu, DKNY</strong>. The thirty-year-old also poses in the most famous fashion magazines: “<strong>Vogue</strong>“, “<strong>Marie Claire</strong>“, “<strong>Glamour</strong>“.</p>
<p>Model with perfect shapes, Emily Ratajkowski fights for a <strong> non-stereotypical canon of beauty </strong>, stating that she dreams of inclusive catwalks for all sizes and heights.</p>
