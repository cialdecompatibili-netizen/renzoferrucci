<p>He is one of the most appreciated voices by <em>Millennials</em>, who see themselves in his love-filled lyrics and dance to the rhythm of his summer hits.</p>
<p>Not just a performer: <strong>Irama</strong> captures glances - and ensures an <strong>ever-larger slice of enamored fans</strong>! – also thanks to her <strong>look</strong> that is never predictable, never repetitive, accompanied by a<em> hairstyling</em> that ranges from the wild bob to the reinterpretation of the <strong>mullet</strong> of the Eighties. His outfits are not recommended for anyone who doesn't like to be noticed: after all, audacity is one of the qualities of this local talent.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Biografia"></span>Biography<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Filippo Maria Fanti</strong>, better known as <strong>Irama</strong> (anagram of his second name and “<strong>ritmo</strong>” in the Malay language) was born in Carrara on 20 December 1995.</p>
<p>He had just finished primary school when the entire Fanti family moved from Carrara to <strong>Monza</strong>. The young singer took his first steps in the world of music, immediately distinguishing himself for his eclectic and "encyclopedic" streak: at an early age he approached our "involved" music with curiosity, loving sacred monsters like <strong>De André</strong> or<strong> Guccini</strong>, yet the genre with which he put himself to the test right from the start was <strong>rap</strong>.</p>
<p>Between 2014 and 2015 the Tuscan pigeonholed a series of important collaborations, among which we remember <strong> Valerio Sgargi </strong> and the duo then composed of <strong> Benji </strong> and <strong> Fede </strong>. In 2016 he entered the Olympus of Sanremo Nuove Proposte, ranking seventh in the final ranking with the single <em><strong>Cosa restarà</strong></em>.</p>
<p>It is the end of the year 2017 when Irama, fresh from a lot of experience and looking for a showcase in which to show his talent to a wider segment of the public, decides to take part in the well-known talent show “<strong>Amici di Maria De Filippi</strong>“. During this edition the singer wins and seizes the more unique than rare opportunity to manage and implement his success.</p>
<p>The showcase offered by Mediaset and the success of the single <em><strong>Nera</strong></em> allow Irama this time to enter the Ariston Theater from the entrance reserved for the Bigs. In 2019 he received unanimous acclaim with the poignant <em><strong>The girl with the tin heart</strong></em>, which ranked seventh.&nbsp; The ballad is inspired by the true story of <strong>Martina Nasoni</strong>, winner of Big Brother whom Irama met in Salento; he was so struck by the story of the young woman (who wears a pacemaker) that he wrote the song together with <strong>Giuseppe Colonnelli</strong>.</p>
<p>The Sanremo experience of 2019 will not be the only one for the singer-songwriter. In 2021 Irama tries again, presenting the single <em><strong>La genesi del tuo colore</strong></em> during the event, which marks a turning point in the artist's musical style, more leaning towards the <strong>urban</strong>. A Sanremo that Irama hardly forgets: <strong>he ranks fifth despite never having sung live in theatre</strong>, due to the isolation imposed on him by the restrictions to stem the spread of <strong>Covid-19</strong>. In his place, the host Amadeus decides to broadcast the recordings of the rehearsals in the theater.</p>
<p>In 2022 Irama rises one place in the final Sanremo ranking, singing - this time live! – an intense one <em><strong>Wherever you will be</strong> </em>and managing to return to the Ariston stage during the evening dedicated to covers <strong>Gianluca Grignani</strong>.</p>
<p>The singer's most recent single, <em><strong>5 drops</strong></em> (feat. <strong>Rkomi</strong>) is already <strong>gold record</strong>.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Interviste"></span>Interviews<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>During the numerous <strong>interviews</strong> released on TV and in the press <strong>Irama</strong> has always told the genesis of his songs with sensitivity and without frills. During the interview given to Mara Venier on “<strong>Domenica In</strong>” the day after the end of the 2022 Sanremo edition Fanti explained how <em>Ovunque ordini</em> was born, underlining that the song is dedicated “<em>To a person who no longer exists</em>“.</p>
<p>"<em>I was on a terrace in Salento. It was a bit of a bad time for me, dark... <strong>I couldn't write</strong></em> - admitted the singer-songwriter who, having moved away from the group of musicians who accompanied him, began to - <em>look at the sky, look at the earth...I started writing a sort of poem to this person, I started talking to her</em>".</p>
<p><strong>Clearness</strong> and <strong>emotion</strong> often shine through in Irama's interviews, as feisty on stage as he is kind and delicate in his discussions with hosts and journalists. An approach that conquers young but also older people, who underline its politeness and sweetness.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Influenze_musicali"></span>Musical influences<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Although his detractors label him only as the <strong> singer of summer hits </strong> - both listened to and hated - there is, in reality, much more behind the artist Irama.</p>
<p>It's undeniable: the success of popular hits like <em>Nera</em> and <strong><em>Mediterranea</em></strong> is there for all to see. The boy, however, does not want to be classified in just one gender. Irama explains it in depth in 2020, following the release of his EP “<strong>Crepe</strong>“.</p>
<p>"<em>I'm not a rapper, a songwriter or a rock star. <strong>None of us can be contained in a single "genre", not even my music</strong>. From an emotional point of view, cracks are what happens to us and makes us grow. Cracks is what I am today: <strong>a set of influences that I don't like to catalogue</strong>. I like to be influenced by other cultures and bring them into my music</em>".</p>
<p>In Irama's works we quickly move from the sensual <strong> Latin rhythm </strong> to the most romantic of love songs until reaching rhythms closer to rap: all this testifies to the "hunger" for musical knowledge of such a young performer.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Preferenze_stilistiche_di_Irama"></span>Irama's stylistic preferences<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Like any boy of his age, Irama is rather attentive to <strong>current trends</strong> not only in terms of hairstyling, but also and above all in terms of <strong>fashion</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>The boy sets the trend</strong>: thumbs down for sneakers and tracksuits, green light for<strong> jewels, leather jackets, eclectic combinations</strong>. His style is often compared to that of former One Direction <strong>Harry Stiles</strong>, although "our" <strong>Irama</strong> could be considered even more "extreme" in the choice of outfits to wear. In fact, the final result of the singer's total look - clothing plus hairstyling - often borders on "<strong>pirate</strong>" and sometimes has almost "<strong>shamanic</strong>" references!</p>
<p>“<strong><em>I only wear things that make me feel comfortable and that are in tune with my taste, that speak about me</em></strong>“, he admitted in an interview dated 2020 given to the well-known magazine <strong>GQ Italia</strong>. A mantra that often creates rather bright contrasts between the songs he brings to the stages of festivals and shows and the looks sported by the boy born in 1995 and curated for him by the stylist <strong> Simone Rutigliano </strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Hedi Slimane, Haider Ackermann, Dries Van Noten, Saint Laurent, Givenchy</strong> and <strong>Celine</strong> are just some of the <em>fashion brands</em> favored by the singer-songwriter, who is not afraid to dare by focusing on <strong>spotted patterns</strong>, sweaters that resemble the texture of his grandmother's classic <strong>doilies</strong>, transparencies, bandanas and an appeal spasmodic to <strong>black</strong>. Details that make the difference, but which overall give a <strong>sophisticated look</strong>.</p>
