<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Moda_mare_i_costumi_piu_trendy_del_2023"></span>Swimwear fashion: the trendiest swimsuits of 2023<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Summer is upon us and with it the desire for sun, sea and relaxation. But before diving into the crystal clear water, you need to think about what to wear to feel beautiful and comfortable. The 2023 swimwear offers you a wide choice of swimsuits, from classic models to the most original and innovative ones. Let's see together what the trendiest costumes of 2023 are and how to combine them with style.</p>
<h3><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Bikini"></span>Bikini<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>
<p>The bikini is the swimsuit par excellence, the one that cannot be missing from your summer wardrobe. The bikini allows you to tan at your best and enhance your curves. You can choose between different types of bikinis, based on your taste and body shape. Here are some of the most popular models for 2023:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Triangle bikini</strong>: it is the simplest and most versatile model, suitable for all types of breasts. You can opt for a triangle bikini with ties on the sides, which allows you to adjust the fit and create a V-shaped effect that slims the figure. Or you can choose a triangle bikini with an underbust band, which offers you greater support and comfort. The triangle bikini pairs well with any type of brief, from the most opaque to the most high-cut.</li>
<li><strong>Bandeau bikini</strong>: it is the ideal model for those with small or medium breasts, because it enhances and lifts them. The bandeau bikini can be plain or enriched with ruffles, fringes, ruches or other details that add volume and movement. The bandeau bikini pairs well with a high-waisted bottom, which emphasizes the waist and lengthens the legs.</li>
<li><strong>Underwired bikini</strong>: it is the perfect model for those with large breasts, because it supports and shapes it. The underwire bikini can have a balcony or heart shape, which highlights the décolleté. The underwire bikini goes well with a classic or Brazilian bottom, which balances the proportions of the body.</li>
</ul>
<h3><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Costumi_interi"></span>One-piece swimsuits<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h3>
<p>The one-piece swimsuit has come back into fashion in recent years, thanks to its elegance and versatility. The one-piece swimsuit can be worn both on the beach and out of the water, as a bodysuit to match with trousers or skirts. You can choose between different types of one-piece swimsuits, based on your taste and body shape. Here are some of the trendiest models for 2023:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>One-piece swimsuit with cut-out</strong>: this is the model that leaves some parts of the body uncovered, creating plays of full and empty spaces. The one-piece swimsuit with cut-out can have openings on the front, sides or back, which give sensuality and dynamism to the look. The one-piece swimsuit with cut-out is suitable for those with a toned and proportionate body.</li>
<li><strong>One-piece swimsuit with plunging neckline</strong>: it is the model that highlights the breasts, thanks to a plunging neckline that reaches up to the navel. The one-piece swimsuit with a deep neckline can be plain or decorated with laces, rings or other elements that draw attention to the décolleté. The one-piece swimsuit with a deep neckline is suitable for those who have medium or large breasts and want to enhance it.</li>
<li><strong>One-piece swimsuit with print</strong>: it is the model that stands out for its colors and patterns, which make the look lively and original. The printed one-piece swimsuit can have geometric, floral, animalier or tropical patterns, which recall the spirit of summer. The one-piece swimsuit with print is suitable for those who want to give a touch of personality to their outfit.</li>
</ul>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="I_consigli_per_scegliere_il_costume_giusto_per_te"></span>Advice for choosing the right swimsuit for you<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Now that you know what the trendiest swimsuits of 2023 are, you need to know how to choose the right one for you. In fact, it is not enough to follow fashion, but you must also take into account your body shape, your strengths and those to improve. Here are some tips for choosing the right swimsuit for you, based on your body shape:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hourglass body shape</strong>: If you have an hourglass body shape, you're in luck, because you can wear practically any type of swimsuit. The important thing is that the costume respects your proportions and highlights your waist. You can opt for a triangle or bandeau bikini, or for a one-piece swimsuit with cut-out or plunging neckline.</li>
<li><strong>Triangle physique</strong>: if you have a triangle physique, you must try to balance the upper and lower parts of the body, enhancing the breasts and minimizing the hips. You can opt for a bikini with underwire or with voluminous details on the bra, or for a one-piece swimsuit with a plunging neckline or a print.</li>
<li><strong>Reverse triangle physique</strong>: if you have a reverse triangle physique, you must try to balance the upper and lower parts of the body, enhancing the hips and minimizing the shoulders. You can opt for a triangle or bandeau bikini, or for a one-piece swimsuit with cut-out or V-neck.</li>
<li><strong>Rectangle physique</strong>: if you have a rectangle physique, you must try to create curves and emphasize the waistline. You can opt for a bikini with ties or a band under the breast, or for a one-piece swimsuit with cut-out or print.</li>
<li><strong>Apple-shaped body</strong>: if you have an apple-shaped body, you must try to slim down the abdominal area and enhance your legs and breasts. You can opt for an underwired bikini or high-waisted briefs, or for a one-piece swimsuit with a plunging neckline or a print.</li>
</ul>
