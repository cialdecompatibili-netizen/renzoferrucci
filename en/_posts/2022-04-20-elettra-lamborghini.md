<p>She is an heiress, but incredibly nice and witty. <strong>Elettra Lamborghini</strong> is a star with a thousand facets, loved by adults and children alike not so much for her singing skills (much <strong>slaughtered by industry critics</strong>, despite her hits being danced and listened to throughout Italy) but for her bubbly character and her eccentric style, to say the least.</p>
<p>Over the course of her career, the young woman has racked up countless <strong>TV appearances</strong>, a participation in the <strong>Sanremo Festival</strong> and has also given her leopard "touch" to the glittering universe of fashion, designing a hyper-bold collection for the underwear brand <strong>Yamamay</strong>, but not only that.</p>
<p>Let's find out more about the curiosities and career of the toughest VIP in our <em>showbiz</em>!</p>

<p>Elettra Lamborghini is the daughter of <strong>Luisa Peterlongo</strong> and the entrepreneur <strong>Tonino Lamborghini</strong>, as well as granddaughter of the famous entrepreneur <strong>Ferruccio Lamborghini</strong>. Despite coming from a wealthy family, the multifaceted artist did not limit herself to resting on her laurels, on the contrary.</p>
<p>The star immediately took his first steps in the Milanese nightclubs, later arriving on television. In 2015 she was hosted at “<strong>Chiambretti Night</strong>” before becoming a well-known face of the <strong>MTV</strong> reality shows: “<strong>Supershore</strong>” and above all “<strong>Riccanza</strong>” made her popular with an increasingly wider audience.</p>
<p>Followed by the Spanish version of “<strong>Big Brother VIP</strong>” and “<strong>Geordie Shore</strong>“, which brings Elettra to Great Britain. A volcanic personality and often prone to confrontation, Lamborghini also took part in “<strong>Acapulco Shore</strong>” before hosting, again for MTV, “<strong>Ex on the beach Italia</strong>“.</p>
<p>Not just parsley of the small screen: Elettra Lamborghini also loves <strong>music</strong>. After a collaboration in 2017 with the rapper <strong>Gué Pequeno</strong> and the trapper <strong>Sfera Ebbasta</strong> in the song <strong><em>Lamborghini RMX</em></strong>, in 2018 the heiress debuted in the world of seven notes with the Spanish-language single <em><strong>Pem Pem</strong></em>.</p>
<p>It's 2019: Elettra Lamborghini covers the role of coach in the well-known Rai talent show "<strong>The Voice of Italy</strong>". In 2020 he arrives at <strong>Sanremo</strong>, where he competes with the single <strong><em>Musica (and the rest disappears)</em></strong>; in the summer he releases his new single in collaboration with <strong>Giusy Ferreri</strong>, <em>La isla</em>.</p>
<p>Lamborghini's 2021 is divided between television (as a commentator on "<strong>The Island of the Famous</strong>") and music, with the release of <em>Pistolero</em>. He closes a successful year with the hosting of “<strong>Miss Italia</strong>” in December 2021.</p>
<p>Passionate about horses, Elettra Lamborghini is an expert jockey. He also took part in some productions for web TV.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Interviste"></span>Interviews<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Elettra Lamborghini often lends herself to a series of <strong>interviews</strong> on television programs, on the radio or on the web.</p>
<p>The young artist is not afraid to bare herself: she demonstrates this in an interview given to <strong>Radio Deejay</strong>, where Lamborghini lends herself with irony and liveliness to the questions of the hosts <strong>Linus</strong> and <strong>Nicola Savino</strong>. The heiress easily talks about private life and career, underlining that her work often leads her to travel abroad and recounting<strong> the beginnings</strong> of her adventure in the complex world of the star system.</p>
<p>Despite not being interested in a television career, the then 18-year-old Elettra Lamborghini was directed down that path by her very first manager. "<em>He told me that I would be the little goose that laid the golden eggs and put me on television</em> - recalls the singer of Pem Pem - <em>My parents are very scared and wondered what I had done to be on TV. I, on the other hand, was calm: I never do things like that</em>".</p>
<p>Elettra Lamborghini's interview style is simple, direct; nothing set or prepared. The woman amazes with her frankness and her absolute sincerity in telling her story.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Vita_privata"></span>Private life<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Elettra Lamborghini is openly <strong>bisexual</strong>: before marrying her boyfriend <strong>Afrojack</strong> she had in fact flirted with two roommates from “<strong>Geordie Shore</strong>“, Chloe and Marnie.</p>
<p>The heiress' great love, however, is the aforementioned Dutch DJ Afrojack. The two, who got officially engaged in 2018, got married two years later. The <strong>wedding</strong>, organized by the popular wedding planner <strong>Enzo Miccio</strong>, took place on Lake Como on <strong>26 September 2020</strong>. The heiress' sister, <strong>Genevra Lamborghini</strong>, was notably absent from the event: the reasons were never disclosed.</p>
<p>Elettra Lamborghini loves animals very much: she owns <strong>five horses</strong> and more than <strong>thirty dogs</strong>!</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Preferenze_stilistiche_di_Elettra_Lamborghini"></span>Stylist preferences of Elettra Lamborghini<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>If we had to apostrophize the <strong>style</strong> of the<em> queen of twerking</em> in just one word, “<strong>excess</strong>” would be the right one!</p>
<p>Elettra Lamborghini is not exactly a woman who goes unnoticed. <strong>Dizzying necklines, transparencies, leopard prints</strong>: everything or almost everything was worn with absolute nonchalance by the singer and television star, ranging from the most sporty outfits to seductive evening dresses for prestigious red carpets.</p>
<p>Elettra's looks can shock but never leave you indifferent and her taste in clothing is particularly loved by teenagers. This is why, in addition to having collaborated with <a href="https://www.yamamay.com/it_it/elettra-lamborghini-for-yamamay-collection?page=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Yamamay</a>&nbsp; for a young and cheeky line of underwear, Lamborghini has launched its own clothing line designed for newborns, girls and teenagers.</p>
<p>Finally, among the performer's <em>must haves</em> we find the <strong>shoes</strong>: Elettra has an infinite collection of every type of shoe.</p>
