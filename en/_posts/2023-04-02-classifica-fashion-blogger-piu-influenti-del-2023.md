<h2>Who are the most followed and influential fashion bloggers of 2023? Let's discover our top 10 together.</h2>
<p>The world of fashion is constantly evolving, and fashion bloggers have become real opinion leaders who dictate trends and styles. But who are the most followed and influential fashion bloggers of 2023? We have drawn up our ranking based on criteria such as the number of followers, collaborations with brands, quality of content and social impact. Here is our top 10.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Chiara Ferragni</strong>. The undisputed queen of fashion bloggers is still her, Chiara Ferragni. With over 30 million followers on Instagram, a fashion and lifestyle empire that bears her name, a dream family and a fairytale life, Chiara is an icon of style and success that knows no crisis. Her ability to reinvent herself and anticipate trends makes her one of the most admired and imitated influencers in the world.</li>
<li><strong>Aimee Song</strong>. The founder of Song of Style is one of the most famous and appreciated fashion bloggers internationally. With over 6 million followers on Instagram, Aimee Song is a true fashion and design expert, collaborating with the most prestigious brands and participating in the most important fashion weeks. His style is elegant and sophisticated, but also original and fun.</li>
<li><strong>Camila Coelho</strong>. The beautiful Brazilian is one of the most followed and influential fashion bloggers in South America. With over 9 million followers on Instagram, Camila Coelho is a source of inspiration for many women who love her glamorous and sensual, but also practical and versatile style. Camila Coelho is also a beauty guru, who shares her beauty secrets and make-up tutorials.</li>
<li><strong>Leonie Hanne</strong>. German Leonie Hanne is one of the hottest fashion bloggers of the moment. With over 4 million followers on Instagram, Leonie Hanne is a globetrotter who travels the world to discover the latest trends and the most evocative places. His style is colorful and lively, but also refined and elegant. Leonie Hanne is also a business woman who has launched her own line of clothing and accessories.</li>
<li><strong>Blair Eadie</strong>. The founder of Atlantic-Pacific is one of the most veteran and beloved fashion bloggers on the scene. With over 1 million followers on Instagram, Blair Eadie is a true fashion expert who knows how to mix vintage and modern pieces, creating always impeccable and original looks. Her style is feminine and romantic, but also bold and creative.</li>
<li><strong>Negin Mirsalehi</strong>. The beautiful Dutch woman is one of the most followed and influential fashion bloggers in the Middle East. With over 7 million followers on Instagram, Negin Mirsalehi is a successful woman who created her own brand of honey-based hair products, Gisou. Her style is chic and sophisticated, but also sexy and trendy.</li>
<li><strong>Julie Sariñana</strong>. The founder of Sincerely Jules is one of the nicest and brightest fashion bloggers on the internet. With over 5 million followers on Instagram, Julie Sariñana is a true Californian who loves the sun, the sea and nature. His style is fresh and casual, but also refined and refined. Julie Sariñana is also a traveler who shares her adventures and discoveries.</li>
<li><strong>Chriselle Lim</strong>. The founder of The Chriselle Factor is one of the most elegant and refined fashion bloggers on the internet. With over 1 million followers on Instagram, Chriselle Lim is a Korean-American living in Los Angeles who collaborates with the most exclusive brands and the most prestigious magazines. His style is minimalist and sophisticated, but also trendy and innovative.</li>
<li><strong>Caroline Daur</strong>. The young German is one of the most promising and talented fashion bloggers of the moment. With over 3 million followers on Instagram, Caroline Daur is a model and influencer who has conquered the public with her glamorous and bold, but also sweet and feminine style. Caroline Daur is also an athlete who loves fitness and well-being.</li>
<li><strong>Chiara Nasti</strong>. The Neapolitan is one of the most followed and influential fashion bloggers in Italy. With over 2 million followers on Instagram, Chiara Nasti is a successful girl who created her own clothing and accessories brand, Nasty Gal. Her style is sexy and provocative, but also fun and ironic.</li>
</ol>
<p>This was our ranking of the most followed and influential fashion bloggers of 2023. Do you agree with our choices? Who are your favorite fashion bloggers? Let us know in the comments.</p>
