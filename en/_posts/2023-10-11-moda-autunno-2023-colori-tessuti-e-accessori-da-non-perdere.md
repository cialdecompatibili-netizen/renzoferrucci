<p>Autumn is upon us and with it the desire to renew our wardrobe with the most popular garments and accessories of the season. But what are the autumn 2023 fashion trends that we cannot ignore? Which colours, fabrics and styles will make us feel in step with the times and with our personality? In this article we reveal everything you need to know about the fashion news of autumn 2023, from the catwalks to the shop windows, to always be at the top.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Colori"></span>Colors<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>The colors of autumn 2023 are warm, intense and rich in shades. Among the dominant shades we find <strong>red</strong>, which burns on dresses, coats, bags and shoes, giving a touch of passion and vitality to our look. Red pairs perfectly with <strong>black</strong>, the great winter classic, which returns to dress elegant and sophisticated, but also sporty and casual garments. Black also goes well with <strong>white</strong>, creating graphic and chic contrasts. Other colors that stand out are <strong>green</strong>, available in different shades from emerald to military, <strong>blue</strong>, from navy to cobalt, and <strong>purple</strong>, from lavender to plum. There is no shortage of earthy <strong> colors </strong>, such as brown, beige, camel and mustard, which recall nature and fallen leaves. Finally, for those who love to dare, there are <strong>metallic</strong> colors, such as gold, silver and bronze, which illuminate outfits with futuristic flashes.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Tessuti"></span>Fabrics<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>The fabrics of autumn 2023 are soft, enveloping and comfortable. Among the most used materials we find <strong>wool</strong>, which makes its way into oversized sweaters, cocoon coats, pleated skirts and palazzo trousers. Wool is also mixed with other yarns, such as <strong>cachemire</strong>, <strong>mohair</strong> and <strong>alpaca</strong>, to create warm and soft garments. Another protagonist fabric is <strong>denim</strong>, which returns to being a wardrobe must-have, both in a classic and revisited version. Denim is worn on jackets, shirts, skirts and trousers, but also on dresses and accessories. For those looking for a touch of elegance and refinement, there are <strong>noble</strong> fabrics, such as <strong>silk</strong>, <strong>velvet</strong> and <strong>satin</strong>, which give shine and fluidity to the garments. Finally, for those who love sustainability, there are <strong>ecological</strong> and <strong>ethical</strong> fabrics, such as <strong>organic cotton</strong>, <strong>natural linen</strong> and <strong>synthetic fur</strong>, which respect the environment and animals.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Accessori"></span>Accessories<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>The accessories of autumn 2023 are original, functional and versatile. Among the essential elements we find bags and shoes, which complete our look with style and personality. Bags are becoming large and roomy, to contain everything we need in everyday life. The shapes are geometric and minimalist, the colors are neutral or bright, the materials are soft or rigid. The shoes, on the other hand, are made small and delicate, to enhance femininity and grace. The toes are rounded or tapered, the heels are low or medium, the soles are thin or thick. Among the most popular models are ballerinas, slingbacks, Mary Janes and moccasins. Other accessories that cannot be missing are hats, which protect from the cold and add a touch of class to our outfit. Hats come in different shapes and sizes, from berets to berets, from fedoras to wide-brimmed hats. Finally, to enrich our look with precious and bright details, there are jewels, which become large and showy, but also delicate and discreet. Among the trendiest jewels there are necklaces, earrings, bracelets and rings, made with metals, stones, pearls and crystals.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Considerazioni_finali"></span>Final considerations<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Autumn 2023 offers us varied, rich and interesting fashion, which allows us to express our personality and our taste with quality garments and accessories. Whether you love bright colors or neutral tones, soft fabrics or rigid materials, large accessories or small details, you will be able to find your perfect combination among the fashion trends of autumn 2023. The important thing is to feel comfortable with what you wear and have fun creating your personal style.</p>
